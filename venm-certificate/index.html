Contaminated Land

VENM certificates and soil classification — Sydney and NSW

A VENM (Virgin Excavated Natural Material) certificate confirms that excavated soil or rock is uncontaminated and can be reused or transported off-site without chemical testing or an EPA licence. Getex provides VENM assessment and certification for construction and civil projects across Sydney and NSW, helping builders, developers and councils manage spoil disposal efficiently and in compliance with NSW EPA requirements.

What is VENM?

Defined under the NSW Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997.

Under the NSW Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997, VENM is defined as natural material such as clay, gravel, sand, soil or rock fines that has been excavated from areas not contaminated by industrial, commercial, mining or agricultural activities, and that does not contain sulfidic ores, soils or any other waste. Material that has been stockpiled, processed or potentially impacted cannot be classified as VENM.

When do you need a VENM certificate?

Before spoil leaves site.

A VENM certificate is required when excavated material is to be transported off-site for reuse at another location. It is commonly required by councils, receiving sites and project managers before spoil can be accepted.

Common project types

  • Basement excavations
  • Road and infrastructure works
  • Tunnel spoil
  • Civil earthworks
  • Development sites
How Getex assesses and certifies VENM

A defensible assessment, start to certificate.

  1. 01

    Reviews the historical and current land use of the excavation site

  2. 02

    Assesses whether any contaminating activities have occurred on or adjacent to the site

  3. 03

    Considers naturally occurring asbestos risk using SafeWork NSW mapping

  4. 04

    Prepares a certificate in accordance with the NSW EPA template

Where the classification is not straightforward, Getex can undertake targeted soil sampling and testing to support the assessment.

Who we work with

Builders, contractors, developers and councils.

Getex provides VENM assessments for builders, civil contractors, developers, councils and infrastructure operators across Sydney and NSW. The service is commonly required as part of broader contaminated land or waste classification programs.
