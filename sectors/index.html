Sectors

Who we work with

Getex supports organisations responsible for managing environmental and occupational risk across projects, facilities and multi-site assets.

01

Government, Councils & Public Assets

Government authorities and councils responsible for public infrastructure, facilities and community assets.

Typical work includes

  • Asbestos surveys, registers and ongoing management across diverse building portfolios
  • Contaminated land and environmental assessment for public works, open space, infrastructure and redevelopment
  • Investigation and testing of environmental hazards across public and outdoor environments
  • VENM certificates and soil classification for infrastructure and civil works
02

Health, Education & Defence

Sensitive and high-compliance environments where safety, continuity of operations and regulatory requirements are critical.

Typical work includes

  • Asbestos and hazardous materials management within hospitals, schools and secure environments
  • Monitoring programs aligned with site use, occupancy and access constraints
  • Assessment and ongoing monitoring supporting safe operation of facilities, including air, water and environmental quality
03

Construction & Built Environment

Builders, civil contractors, project managers, architects and consultants delivering construction, refurbishment and redevelopment projects.

Typical work includes

  • Asbestos surveys, monitoring and clearance inspections for construction and refurbishment works
  • Contaminated land assessment and site validation
  • Remediation planning, implementation and oversight
04

Property, Facilities & Safety Management

Property, facilities and workplace health and safety teams managing operational sites and portfolios.

Typical work includes

  • Hazardous materials registers and ongoing compliance and risk management
  • Air quality, exposure and personal monitoring
  • Assessment and ongoing monitoring of environmental and occupational exposures and risks, including emerging contaminants
