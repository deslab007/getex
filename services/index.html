Services

Hazardous materials, contaminated land and occupational hygiene

Getex holds over 20 years of expertise in hazardous materials, contaminated land and occupational hygiene across Sydney and New South Wales.

Asbestos & Hazardous Materials

Asbestos surveys, air monitoring, clearance and hazardous materials management, delivered by Licensed Asbestos Assessors and NATA-accredited inspection surveyors.

Core services

  • Asbestos surveys and registers
  • Asbestos removal control plans
  • Licensed Asbestos Assessor services
  • Asbestos air monitoring and clearance inspections
  • Asbestos in soil
  • Hazardous materials surveys and lead testing: lead, SMF, PCBs, ODS
  • Hazardous materials registers and management plans
  • Site supervision: on-site oversight during asbestos removal and hazardous materials works

Where this is applied

  • Government and councils
  • Health, education and defence
  • Property and facilities

Contaminated Land

Risk-based investigation, remediation and validation, including soil, groundwater and landfill gas assessment. Led by a Certified Environmental Practitioner (Site Contamination).

Core services

  • Preliminary Site Investigations (PSI) and Detailed Site Investigations (DSI)
  • Soil, water and sediment sampling
  • Site contamination assessments
  • Waste classification
  • Remedial Action Plans
  • Site validation and management plans
  • Landfill gas and acid sulfate soil assessments
  • Emerging contaminants monitoring, including PFAS screening and assessment
  • Pre-purchase and due diligence environmental assessments
  • VENM certificates and soil classification
  • Groundwater monitoring
  • Underground storage tank (UST) removal validation

Where this is applied

  • Government and councils
  • Construction and built environment

Occupational Hygiene

Exposure monitoring and air quality assessment across dust, silica, noise, lighting, fumes and contaminants. Our airborne contaminant monitoring is NATA-accredited.

Core services

  • Occupational exposure monitoring
  • Dust and particulate monitoring, including respirable crystalline silica
  • Dust deposit gauges: passive dust deposition monitoring
  • Indoor and outdoor air-quality assessments
  • Noise assessment: workplace noise exposure monitoring
  • Lighting assessment: workplace lighting and illuminance assessment
  • Water-quality testing
  • Mould investigations
  • Clandestine drug laboratory assessments

Where this is applied

  • Property and facilities
  • Construction and built environment
  • Health, education and defence

Training

Practical, on-site training tailored to staff, contractors or the public, across asbestos, hazardous materials, contaminated land and occupational hygiene.

  • Asbestos awareness training
  • Hazardous materials identification training
  • Site-specific inductions for hazardous materials and contamination risks
