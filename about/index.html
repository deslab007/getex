About

Independent environmental and occupational hygiene consultancy.

Getex is an environmental and occupational hygiene consultancy specialising in hazardous materials, contaminated land and occupational hygiene.

Operating since 2005, Getex works across public assets, sensitive environments, construction projects and property portfolios.

Getex has offices in Sydney and the Central Coast, with projects delivered across regional New South Wales and interstate.

What we bring

Integrity. Flexibility. Quality.

The principles that shape how we work with our clients.

Integrity

We provide independent advice, communicate clearly on risks and requirements, and take a transparent approach to our work and reporting.

Flexibility

We adapt to project requirements, site conditions and client systems, working within operational environments, staged works and evolving scopes.

Quality

Our work is underpinned by accredited systems, consistent methodology and a focus on reliable outcomes.

We remember your sites

With over 20 years of project history, many of our clients return to us knowing we already hold their site records, previous surveys and monitoring results. We pick up where we left off. No re-briefing, no lost context, just consistent advice from people who know your asset.

Accreditations

Accredited, independent, defensible.

  • NATA Accreditation (ISO/IEC 17020) – Asbestos & Hazardous Materials Inspections and Surveys
  • NATA Accreditation (ISO/IEC 17020) – Airborne Contaminant Monitoring
  • ISO 9001 – Quality Management Systems
  • ISO 45001 – Occupational Health & Safety Management Systems
  • Certified Environmental Practitioner, Site Contamination Specialist (CEnvP)

Why accreditation matters

Recognised methodology and reporting that stands up to regulatory and audit scrutiny. Getex is one of a small number of independent environmental consultancies in NSW accredited by NATA for both hazardous materials inspections and airborne contaminant monitoring.
